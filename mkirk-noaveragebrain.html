No Average Brain

Leadership for every kind of mind.

About the Book

The book builds one argument in two moves: first the case that dyslexic, autistic, ADHD, and anxious-brained thinkers are exactly the minds a volatile, AI-shaped world now needs, then the system for leading them without asking them to translate themselves first. It's carried by Matt Goode's own record – the leadership meetings he sat through in silence, the son he pulled out of a school that couldn't see him, the founders and executives he's since coached through the redesign. Two decades of teaching sit underneath it: watching, year after year, which kids got flagged as deficient and which ones the flag turned out to be wrong about.

It replaces "make room for difference" with an operating system built for cognitive range specifically – CREATE, VOICES, the 6C Compass, DELEGATE.

Most books in this category make either the moral case for accommodation or the business case for hiring differently, rarely both, and rarely from someone who's run the consulting himself. This one also does something the category mostly skips: it names AI's arrival as the moment the old barrier actually falls, and spells out what that means for who gets to lead next.

Direct and personal rather than academic – closer to a leader thinking out loud with you than a management manual. Goode moves between hard data – attrition numbers, masking research, WEF skills forecasts – and what those numbers cost specific people, including his own family.

For readers of Quiet and The Dyslexic Advantage – and sits closest to Goode's own CLUTCH: the same operating system, now pointed at the specific minds it was quietly built to include all along.
