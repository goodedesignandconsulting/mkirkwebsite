Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no ad networks. If you never type anything into it, it never learns who you are.

How visits are counted

Visits are measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is cookieless, it stores nothing on your device, and it reports aggregate figures such as page views and referrers rather than anything identifying you. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, the script does not load at all.

Where the site lives

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its servers process the ordinary request information needed to deliver a page, including your IP address, under GitHub’s own privacy terms. Those logs are not received or retained here. Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google and Google receives your IP address.

The mailing list

The newsletter, chapter downloads and waitlists are handled by Kit (formerly ConvertKit), which stores your email address and sends the mail. You can unsubscribe from any message and unsubscribing removes you. Your address is not sold, rented or traded, and it is not used to build a profile of you anywhere else.

Advance review copies

If you request an ARC, the name and email you give are used to send you the book, to keep track of who has a copy, and to follow up about a review. That record is kept until the book is published and the review window has closed. See the terms and rights page for what an ARC may and may not be used for.

Links out

Buy links go to Amazon and other retailers, and social links go to Instagram. Once you follow one of those you are on their site under their privacy terms, not this one.

Asking what is held

Write to matt@thatsgoodedesign.com and ask what is held about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction, or ask for it to be deleted. This is one writer and a mailing list, not a bureaucracy.

This site is not directed at children under 13 and no information is knowingly collected from them.
