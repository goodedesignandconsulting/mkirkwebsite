Launching October 1

Read The Wrath of Archie Ellis the day it drops

A novel of Ypres, 1917

Captain Archie Ellis commands B Company at Ypres in 1917. His orders are overridden by a commander who is wrong. He acts outside them — and two hundred and forty-seven men die.

It's a structural retelling of the Iliad at Passchendaele — Achilles's wrath mapped onto the Western Front, the German side rendered at the same depth as the British. Spare, precise, quietly devastating. It doesn't make the slaughter meaningful. It refuses to.
